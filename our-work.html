Women Empowerment

The goal

Help women build lasting economic independence through enterprise, not just employment.

What we do

Entrepreneurship seminars, mentorship, business support and seed-capital initiatives — including support for widows and women starting new businesses.

Who we serve

Women across the communities we work in, with particular attention to widows and single mothers.

03

Health & Wellness

The goal

Bring screening, health education and direct care within reach of communities that don't always have easy access to it.

What we do

Community medical camps, health screening and education, delivered alongside trusted health-sector partners.

Who we serve

Community members in the areas where camps are held.

Impact

921 participants — South B Community Medical Camp, 31 May 2025, delivered with KEMRI, the Betting Control and Licensing Board, and MobiLab Africa.
